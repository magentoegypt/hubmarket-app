# Store listing: English and Arabic drafts

Paste-ready text for **Google Play** and the **App Store**, written only from what the app does
today and from the wording the client approved in the Figma (v3, TR03). Nothing here promises
same-day delivery, free delivery, 24/7 support, card payments or a delivery area: the store does
not set those. Where a sentence depends on an admin setting, it is listed under
[Facts to confirm](#facts-to-confirm-before-publishing) so the text can be trimmed if the setting
is off at launch.

Every block below has its limit in the heading. The counts were checked when this was written;
recount if you edit.

| Field | Google Play | App Store |
|---|---|---|
| Name | 30 | 30 |
| Short description / subtitle | 80 | 30 |
| Full description | 4000 | 4000 |
| Promotional text | – | 170 |
| Keywords | – | 100 (commas, no spaces) |
| What's new / release notes | 500 | 4000 |

**Name.** `Hub Market` in both languages (the logo is Latin). DEV03 renamed the existing listing;
if the client decides on "ME Hub Market", change it here, in `CFBundleDisplayName` and in the
Android label, and keep the store name identical to the icon and the developer account.

**Category.** Shopping on both stores. Skip a secondary category: Health & Fitness and
Lifestyle invite questions the app does not answer (see the privacy document).

### Full description (max 4000)

```
Hub Market brings the stores of a whole marketplace into one app. Browse groceries, furniture, fashion and more from verified vendors, put items from different stores in one cart, and have them delivered to your door.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Browse and search. Open a category, a brand or a store, or search for a product by name.

• Find deals. See today's deals with the discount on each item, and bundle deals that show what you save.

• Know who you are buying from. Every product shows the store that sells it, with its rating. Open a store to see its products, reviews, contact details and policies. When other stores sell the same product, compare their offers.

• One cart, many stores. Order from several stores at once. Your cart and your order show what comes from each store and how many packages to expect.

• Pay on delivery. Choose cash on delivery at checkout. Check out as a guest or with an account.

• Track your orders. See the status, shipments and tracking numbers for each store's package.

• Return an item. Start a return from your order, attach photos, message the store and follow your refund.

• Store credit. See your credit balance and history, top it up, and use it at checkout.

• Save for later. Keep your favourites in your wishlist.

• Sign in your way. Use your email and password, or a one-time code sent to your WhatsApp.

• English and Arabic. Switch the language at any time. In Arabic the whole app turns right to left.

• You stay in control. Update your details, manage your addresses and delete your account inside the app.

Hub Market is a marketplace: each product is sold and shipped by the store shown on its page. Prices are in AED.

Need help? Open the Help centre in the app for answers and to contact us.
```

```
Hub Market يجمع متاجر السوق كلّه في تطبيق واحد. تصفّح البقالة والأثاث والأزياء والمزيد من بائعين موثّقين، وضع منتجات من متاجر مختلفة في سلة واحدة، واستلمها حتى باب بيتك.

• تتبّع طلباتك. شاهد الحالة والشحنات وأرقام التتبّع لطرد كل متجر.

• إرجاع منتج. ابدأ طلب إرجاع من طلبك، وأرفق صورًا، وراسل المتجر، وتابع استرداد المبلغ.

• احفظ ما يعجبك. أضف منتجاتك المفضّلة إلى قائمة المفضّلة.

• سجّل الدخول بالطريقة التي تناسبك. بالبريد الإلكتروني وكلمة المرور، أو برمز يصلك على واتساب.

• أنت المتحكّم. عدّل بياناتك وأدِر عناوينك واحذف حسابك من داخل التطبيق.

Hub Market سوق يضم عدة متاجر: كل منتج يبيعه ويشحنه المتجر المذكور في صفحته. الأسعار بالدرهم الإماراتي.
```

## Facts to confirm before publishing

Each row is a sentence in the listing that is true only if the client's setup makes it true.
If the answer is no, delete the matching bullet (Arabic too).

| # | Sentence | True when | Where to check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "verified stores / vendors" (short description, subtitle, first paragraph) | Sellers are checked before they are approved. The wording is the client's own (Figma welcome screen). | Client |
| 2 | "delivered to your door" | Delivery works for the customers the listing reaches. The current CMS blocks still say "Nationwide across Egypt": fix them, and decide whether the listing should name the UAE or the emirates. | Client, admin CMS |
| 3 | "Pay on delivery" is the only payment named | Cash on delivery is the only method live. If card, Tabby or Tamara arrive (DEV08, DEV09), add them here and to the privacy answers. | `available_payment_methods` |
| 4 | "Return an item … follow your refund" | Returns are on (`hmReturnConfig`) and the seller panel handles them. Do not state a return window: none is set in the app. | Admin › Hub Market App › Feature Flags |
| 5 | "Store credit … top it up" | The Store Credit product exists (the Buy credit card only appears with it) and `credit/general/credit_group` is set. | Admin › Vnecoms Store Credit |
| 6 | "a one-time code sent to your WhatsApp" | The WhatsApp/SMS provider is live on the production server and the WhatsApp number in the CMS is the client's (it is a US number today). | Admin, SmsExtend |
| 7 | "with its rating" | Stores have approved reviews. Test data today. | Live data |
| 8 | "Compare their offers" | Other-seller offers exist for real products. | Live data |

Not in the text on purpose: push notifications (Firebase is not set up), order cancellation (an
admin toggle), delivery times, prices, discounts, "authentic" or "original" guarantees, ratings
figures and any store or product count.
